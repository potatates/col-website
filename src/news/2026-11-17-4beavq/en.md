When the world of antique dealing meets AI interactive drama, a treasure-appraisal adventure once experienced only in a novel gets a whole new format.

COL Group’s AI interactive drama adapted from its classic webnovel IP “Tian Xia Cang Ju” is now live, debuting on both Fanqie Novel and NetEase Tianma. Combining a suspenseful antique-appraisal story with AI-powered interactive gameplay, it puts viewers right inside the story, turning them from spectators into players in an adventure full of twists and challenges.

## A hit IP reimagined in a world of antiques and intrigue

![Su Chen: twelve years of training in the mountains, now stepping into the wider world](2026-11-17-4beavq/2.jpg)

“Tian Xia Cang Ju” is adapted from the popular novel of the same name by author Xiao Jiutu, which built a large readership with its distinctive antique-appraisal theme and gripping plot.

The novel is rated 9.4 on Fanqie Novel and 9.5 on Qimao, where it has more than 8 million in popularity. The audiobook has passed 700 million plays across platforms and reached No. 3 on Ximalaya’s new-release chart, a sign of the IP’s strong pull.

The AI interactive drama keeps the original’s compelling premise, setting the story in an old town lined with antique shops. Su Chen, who spent twelve years training in the mountains, is drawn into a treasure-hunting mystery as soon as he arrives. From battles of wits over real and fake antiques to a long-hidden secret about his origins, a nerve-racking adventure unfolds.

This time, though, you decide where the story goes.

## Branching storylines make the appraisal adventure truly playable

Identifying a coin’s era by its ring, seeing through the secrets of porcelain, hunting for clues among antiques of doubtful authenticity: the novel’s standout moments are now yours to experience firsthand.

![One copper coin, and you can hear its dynasty](2026-11-17-4beavq/3.jpg)

“Tian Xia Cang Ju” features multiple branching storylines and a range of interactions, including key decisions, item collection and building relationships with characters. Players must make choices at critical moments, and each choice shapes how the plot unfolds and can even change the relationships between characters.

AI technology also brings to life the old town’s historic streets, antique markets and vivid characters, immersing players in the thrills and charm of the antiques world.

![Step in, explore and unlock each mystery](2026-11-17-4beavq/4.jpg)

## AI-powered innovation breathes new life into a classic IP

From hit webnovel to AI interactive drama, the launch of “Tian Xia Cang Ju” is another step in COL’s ongoing exploration of combining AI and IP.

In recent years, COL has drawn on its extensive library of original literary IP to apply AI technology in content creation, expanding into AI microdramas, AI animation, AI interactive dramas and other formats so great stories can reach audiences in more ways.

The launch of “Tian Xia Cang Ju” marks a new milestone for COL’s AI interactive content and opens up more possibilities for developing and reinventing classic IP.

From reading the story to cracking the case yourself, the door to the antiques world is now open. “Tian Xia Cang Ju” is available now on Fanqie Novel and NetEase Tianma.
