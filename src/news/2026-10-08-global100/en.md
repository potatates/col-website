At the 5th Global Digital Trade Expo in Hangzhou, Cyzone released its “2026 Digital Trade · China Globalization New Force Top 100” list. Thanks to continued progress in taking microdrama overseas, COL made the list again and was ranked among the “leading” companies.

The list combines questionnaires, interviews, expert review and third-party data checks, scoring companies on the depth of their global footprint, product competitiveness, scale, compliance and brand awareness. Companies are split into “leading” and “growing” groups; the 40 “leading” companies each earned at least USD 15 million overseas in 2025 and have built scale in overseas markets.

## Microdrama going global

The listing reflects COL’s steady progress in global microdrama. Its overseas platform FlareFlow launched in April 2025 and in the same year reached No. 1 on both the US Google Play and App Store free entertainment charts. It has over 33 million registered users across 200+ countries and regions. In 2025, COL’s overseas revenue grew 123.94% year on year to more than 40% of total revenue.

> Going global doesn’t mean watching overseas data from home. You have to enter the local market, understand local users and build local teams.
> — Tong Zhilei, Founder, Chairman and President of COL Group

With IP as its anchor and AI as its sail, COL will keep building a global microdrama ecosystem and bringing great stories to audiences worldwide.
