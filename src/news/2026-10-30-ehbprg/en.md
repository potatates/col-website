The new chapter keeps the series’ warm, heartfelt tone. It follows Xiaohei’s summer vacation as he tries to go to school together with Luo Xiaobai, inviting viewers back into the familiar yet surprising world of The Legend of Hei.

Since its debut in 2011, the animated series “The Legend of Hei” has won wide acclaim, with more than 1 billion views online and ratings of 9.9 on Bilibili and 9.5 on Douban. In 2019, the first animated feature film, “The Legend of Hei,” grossed over RMB 300 million and was selected for the official Contrechamp feature competition at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival in France, taking the series from streaming to the big screen. In 2025, the animated film “The Legend of Hei 2” ranked No. 3 among Douban’s highest-rated Chinese-language films of the year, grossed more than RMB 533 million in mainland China, and was released in cinemas in more than ten countries across Europe, North America, Australia, New Zealand and Asia.

From series to film, and now a new chapter of the series, “The Legend of Hei” keeps extending the life of the IP through continuous content creation. COL Group will keep investing in quality IP operations, helping more great IP get seen and loved.
