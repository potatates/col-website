FlareFlow, the international microdrama platform of COL Group, launched this week on AIS PLAY, the digital entertainment platform of leading Thai telecom operator AIS (Advanced Info Service). The launch brings Thai users a dedicated FlareFlow section offering quality vertical entertainment across languages and genres. Following partnerships with Thailand’s Charoen Pokphand Group and Digicel, a telecom operator in Central and South America, the deal is an important step in expanding FlareFlow’s multi-platform distribution and its presence in Southeast Asia. It is also another example of COL working with overseas telecom operators to distribute dedicated microdrama sections and localize its operations.

AIS is Thailand’s leading provider of telecommunications and digital infrastructure, with businesses spanning mobile, home broadband, enterprise services and digital entertainment. Thailand has a population of around 70 million; as of June 2026, AIS served 53.9 million customers in total, including 47.1 million mobile subscribers. AIS PLAY is one of Thailand’s major digital entertainment services, with a broad local user base and distribution channels, and can further extend content reach through the AIS group’s telecom-centred businesses and ecosystem.

The new FlareFlow-branded section offers locally produced microdramas from the US and Europe, China, South Korea and elsewhere, all with Thai subtitles, spanning romance, drama, fantasy and reality shows. The section is offered as a separate monthly subscription, giving subscribers access on both mobile and TV screens. The first titles include the vertical reality show “Supermodel Me: Make It or Break It”, hosted by well-known Thai TV host, actress and model Cindy Bishop, which brings reality TV to a vertical format.

![](2026-11-05-nd86da/3.jpg)

The partnership pairs FlareFlow’s global content with AIS PLAY’s local reach, laying the groundwork for deeper content cooperation in Thailand. Next, the two companies will explore local Thai content development, creator partnerships, content localization and new mobile entertainment experiences, expanding their collaboration in Thailand and across Southeast Asia.

Built on a hybrid monetization model and multi-platform distribution, FlareFlow continues to grow both its own platform and its presence on leading regional channels such as AIS PLAY, extending its reach and commercial potential. Going forward, COL will keep bringing quality global content together with local channels and creative talent to deepen its presence in overseas markets.
