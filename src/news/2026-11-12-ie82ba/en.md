On the evening of November 10, the 39th China Golden Rooster Awards ceremony and the closing of the 2026 China Golden Rooster and Hundred Flowers Film Festival took place at the Straits Grand Theatre in Xiamen. “The Legend of Hei 2,” an animated feature co-produced by COL Group, received a jury nomination for Best Animated Film.

![](2026-11-12-ie82ba/1.jpg)

This year’s awards drew 209 film entries, with strong competition across every category. Starting this year, the former “Best Art Film” category was officially renamed “Best Animated Film,” and “The Legend of Hei 2” joined a strong group of Chinese animated films on the first nomination list under the new name. As one of COL’s core proprietary IPs, Luo Xiaohei’s appearance once again showed the distinctive appeal of original Chinese animation.

Speaking at the nominee recognition ceremony, producer Cao Zijian reflected: “We drew it frame by frame on the screen, while audiences waited year after year on the other side.” For him, the nomination recognizes not only the creative team but also every viewer who has followed “The Legend of Hei” along the way.

![Producers Cong Fangbing and Cao Zijian of “The Legend of Hei 2” at the recognition ceremony](2026-11-12-ie82ba/2.jpg)

## Standing out on quality in a crowded field

Chinese animated films have flourished in recent years, with a string of breakout hits continually raising market expectations.

At the same time, competition has grown fiercer. From visual effects to storytelling, from character design to emotional resonance, Chinese animation has entered a new stage where quality and creativity are tested on every front.

In this crowded field, “The Legend of Hei 2” keeps the series’ warm, heartfelt style, winning audiences over with nuanced emotion, memorable characters and striking action sequences. Since its release on July 18, 2025, the film has grossed RMB 533 million in mainland China and holds an 8.7 rating on Douban, a success at the box office and with critics alike.

## From the big screen to everyday life

Since the web animation premiered in 2011 and the franchise moved on to feature films, Luo Xiaohei has been with audiences for more than a decade, growing into a widely influential original Chinese animation IP. The TV series on Bilibili built a large following and a strong reputation with its fresh art style and warm storytelling. On October 30 this year, a new TV chapter, “The Legend of Hei: Preschool,” premiered exclusively on Bilibili, picking up the everyday adventures of Xiaohei and Luo Xiaobai after a five-year gap.

Today Luo Xiaohei’s appeal is reaching well beyond the screen. Around this core IP, COL continues to explore film and TV content, brand licensing, merchandise and offline events. It has formed in-depth licensing partnerships with leading brands such as GONG, a Pop Mart label, and Miniso, and several co-branded products sold out on launch, connecting the beloved characters with even more consumers.

In an increasingly competitive animation market, a truly moving story always has the power to endure.

From the big screen to the Golden Rooster stage, Luo Xiaohei’s journey continues, with more surprises to come. COL will keep putting quality content first, bringing more original Chinese IP to the world so great stories can travel further and reach more people.
