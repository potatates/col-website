On October 22, the National Radio and Television Administration (NRTA) announced its selection of outstanding online audiovisual programs for the second quarter of 2026. Of the 39 titles chosen, 14 are microdramas, including “Yi Pin Bu Yi 4: Ge Ju (The Commoner 4: Rival Warlords)”, produced by COL Group.

![](2026-10-28-iwpcxq/1.jpg)

The selection aims to encourage high-quality online audiovisual content and hold up outstanding programs as examples for the industry. Entries are recommended by provincial broadcasting authorities and centrally administered organizations, then reviewed by an NRTA expert panel to produce the final list. Selections are made each quarter, with results announced quarterly.

On October 26, Xinhua News Agency published an overview titled “14 Microdramas Make the List!”, which People’s Daily republished the same day. Looking at subject matter, creative approach, values and who is making the shows, the article traced how the microdrama industry is changing, calling the selected works “14 windows” onto the sector.

On subject matter, Xinhua noted a shift from everyone chasing the same trends to a far wider range of genres. That expansion gives creators more room to express themselves and shows microdrama moving from being driven by instant gratification to being driven by content. On creative approach, quality shows not only in the work but in the attitude behind it: where comment sections were once full of words like “satisfying,” “comeuppance” and “plot twist,” viewers now increasingly talk about shows that “reflect real life” and are “healing and relatable.” On values, microdrama is growing from mere “background snacking” into an art form that can carry meaning, with mainstream values expressed not through preaching but through vivid stories that quietly reach the heart. On creators, mainstream media, professional institutions and government bodies have entered the field, competing alongside commercial production companies, each playing to its strengths.

On the list, Xinhua grouped “Yi Pin Bu Yi 4: Ge Ju” among works “writing the history of family and nation.” The “Yi Pin Bu Yi (The Commoner)” series follows a commoner hero’s survival and choices in a turbulent age. Earlier installments moved from personal livelihood and family duty to court intrigue; in “Ge Ju,” the story’s national storyline broadens further, tying the hero’s choices to a larger historical backdrop.

![](2026-10-28-iwpcxq/2.jpg)

The series has also performed strongly. The first “Yi Pin Bu Yi,” released in 2025, passed 3 billion views across platforms and was one of only two male-oriented microdramas on the Hongguo platform to exceed 3 billion views that year. The series has now surpassed 6 billion views in total. “Yi Pin Bu Yi 4: Ge Ju” premiered exclusively on Hongguo on April 4 this year, reaching a popularity score of over 70.92 million on its first day and topping both the new-release chart and the historical drama chart.

The series has also earned a string of awards. The first “Yi Pin Bu Yi” was named Microdrama of the Year by China TV Variety magazine for 2025, Hit Microdrama of the Year at the 2025 Hongguo Annual Awards, and Most Commercially Valuable Microdrama in the Xinghan Imaging Program at the 16th Beijing International Film Festival, among other honors, and was selected as a key online project of “Beijing Grand Audiovisual.” The third installment, “Yi Pin Bu Yi 3: Chao Tang (The Commoner 3: The Imperial Court),” was included in the Beijing Municipal Radio and Television Bureau’s list of “45 audiovisual must-sees for Spring Festival.” The original webnovel was named to the IP Influence List of the 2025 China Webnovel Influence Rankings by the China Writers Association’s Online Literature Center, and has been added to the China National Archives of Publications and Culture as a digital collection.

Xinhua concluded that the pursuit of quality is a long-distance race that demands patience and focus, and a long race needs a steady supply at the source. COL has built a library of more than 5.6 million digital content titles and over 4 million registered writers, providing an ongoing supply for microdrama development. Going forward, COL will continue to build on its high-quality IP to drive its microdrama business toward higher quality, series development and multi-format adaptation.
