From October 21 to 23, the 19th CLE China Licensing Expo took place at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre. For the third year in a row, COL Group brought its popular animated IP “The Legend of Hei” to the show, at stand W1H15. The stand highlighted the IP’s licensing and cross-brand collaborations as COL continues to explore the commercial value of top Chinese animation IP across a wide range of consumer settings.

One of the world’s leading licensing trade shows, this year’s CLE brought together more than 620 exhibitors and over 2,700 licensed IPs from China and abroad, spanning animation, film and TV, pop culture and more, connecting IP owners with brands.

## New collaborations keep unlocking commercial value

From the big screen into everyday life, Luo Xiaohei is reaching fans in more ways than ever.

Since “The Legend of Hei 2” hit cinemas last year, COL has focused on the IP’s long-tail value between film releases, steadily growing its licensing portfolio across smart devices, food and beverage, sportswear and streetwear, lifestyle retail and consumer electronics. Recently, Luo Xiaohei teamed up with contact lens brand Acuvue and sportswear brand Xtep, bringing the much-loved character into more parts of daily life and opening new ways to engage young consumers.

This year, Luo Xiaohei has also launched collaborations with well-known brands including Vivo, Tastien, MINISO, Vinda and Akko, bringing the character’s charm to a variety of products and experiences.

In collectible toys and merchandise, COL continues to work with partners such as Wandian Wuxian, Wanle Zhuyi, FansZ and Tuyi Puzzle to build out a product range of plush toys, collectibles and creative jigsaw puzzles. Luo Xiaohei is also exploring interactive experiences with brands such as JUSTFOTO, including themed photo booths, AR filters and virtual group photos, extending the IP across online and offline experiences.

These partnerships not only broaden Luo Xiaohei’s commercial reach but also build wider brand awareness as the IP keeps reaching consumers. From content to co-branded products to interactive experiences, Luo Xiaohei continues to show the long-tail value of quality Chinese animation IP.

## Back after five years: new episodes from October 30

Alongside its growing licensing business, the much-anticipated “The Legend of Hei” TV series is about to return.

“The Legend of Hei: Preschool Arc” premieres exclusively on Bilibili at 12:00 on October 30, with new episodes every Friday at 12:00. It is the first new main chapter of the TV animation in five years, following “The Gate of All Beings” arc in 2021.

![](2026-10-23-t34sda/11.jpg)

The new chapter keeps the series’ warm, heartfelt tone. It follows Xiaohei’s summer vacation as he tries to go to school together with Luo Xiaobai, inviting viewers back into the familiar yet surprising world of The Legend of Hei.

From expanding brand partnerships to a new animated chapter, “The Legend of Hei” keeps extending the life of the IP through content creation and diverse licensing working hand in hand. COL will keep investing in quality IP operations and exploring more cross-industry collaborations and commercial opportunities, bringing great stories into more people’s lives.

On October 30, join Luo Xiaohei for a new story!
