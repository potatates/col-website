From October 12 to 15, the 42nd MIPCOM took place at the Palais des Festivals in Cannes, France. COL attended with its international microdrama platform FlareFlow, meeting industry peers from around the world to discuss new opportunities in microdrama.

## Talking microdrama at a global market

MIPCOM is one of the world’s key markets for trading and co-producing film and TV content, drawing more than 10,000 professionals from over 100 countries each year. In 2026 it grew into a broader global content market, with branded entertainment, AIGC, the creator economy and microdrama as headline topics.

> A year ago, microdrama, the creator economy and AI content production were emerging trends. Today they are scalable business models and an increasingly important part of the global content economy.
> — Lucy Smith, Director, MIPCOM

This year MIPCOM expanded its microdrama programme with an invitation-only Global Microdrama Leaders Summit and, for the first time, a dedicated business-matching track for microdrama. From conversations to signed deals, microdrama carried noticeably more weight at the show.

At stand P-1.D62, COL met international buyers and producers to discuss microdrama content and partnerships. Timothy Oh, COL’s General Manager for Southeast Asia and Chief Marketing Officer of FlareFlow, spoke on a microdrama panel alongside Maria Rua Aguete, Head of Media & Entertainment at research firm Omdia, about how the market sees microdrama, what drives its global growth and where the industry is heading.

## Reaching new markets through telecom partners

During the show, FlareFlow announced a multi-market distribution partnership with Digicel, a leading telecom operator in Central and South America and the Caribbean, covering 25 markets. FlareFlow will connect to Digicel’s billing system so users can subscribe with their prepaid balance or add it to their monthly phone bill.

FlareFlow keeps expanding its distribution channels. By plugging into local operators’ billing and distribution networks, it makes subscribing easier for users in emerging markets and brings microdrama to more mobile viewers. Digicel’s broad user base across the region is a natural fit for FlareFlow’s mobile-first content and payment model.

From industry conversations to channel partnerships, COL keeps deepening its work with international partners, connecting microdrama to more markets so audiences everywhere can enjoy great stories more easily.
