Singapore Business Review has announced the winners of its 2026 Management Excellence Awards. Timothy Oh, COL Group’s General Manager for Southeast Asia and Chief Marketing Officer of FlareFlow, was named Outstanding Executive of the Year in the Media & Entertainment category. The award honors business leaders who have excelled in driving growth, meeting industry challenges and leading innovation. The win reflects COL’s progress in global market expansion, AI content production and branded entertainment.

![](2026-11-20-ndl2ag/1.jpg)

Ray Tong, CEO of COL’s international business, said the award recognizes not only Timothy Oh’s leadership but also the collective efforts of the whole team. In 2026, the company has deepened strategic partnerships and broadened its commercial channels, extending its business from content licensing and distribution into content development, AI applications and brand partnerships for a more diversified portfolio.

![](2026-11-20-ndl2ag/2.jpg)

In content development and global distribution, COL continues to explore new forms of vertical entertainment and is growing in international markets through its global microdrama platform FlareFlow. On the content side, the company keeps broadening the genres and formats of vertical entertainment. On the distribution side, drawing on a library of more than 5,000 microdramas across multiple languages and genres, it is deepening partnerships with international telecom operators and streaming platforms to widen its global distribution channels.

In AI content production, COL has launched COL AI Studios, offering end-to-end AI production services covering original IP development, adaptation of existing entertainment IP and content localization. COL AI Studios combines AI technology with the company’s content-creation expertise to give rights holders and platform partners faster, more efficient production and adaptation support, while its localization services help established IP enter new countries and regions.

In branded entertainment, COL is exploring new models of commercial partnership. On October 27, together with V47 Entertainment and VIRTUE Asia, the company hosted Inside Microdramas, a content showcase in Singapore, where it unveiled its 2027 vertical entertainment slate. The slate includes proven hit microdramas from its existing library, Korean webtoon and webnovel IP developed with CJ Group, and new original entertainment projects. Beyond traditional advertising and product placement, brands can also take part in developing and financing entertainment IP and explore co-ownership of IP rights, taking brand–entertainment partnerships to a deeper level.

Looking ahead, COL will keep expanding in global markets and deepening its content, technology and commercial partnerships, creating more opportunities for rights holders, brands and platform partners worldwide.
