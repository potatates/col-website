COL’s strategic partnership with Thailand’s CP Group has reached a new milestone: FlareFlow Mini is now live on TATANG by TrueID, a local Thai microdrama platform, with its own dedicated section bringing localized vertical microdramas to Thai viewers.

It is a key step in turning the partnership, announced in August, into action.

CP Group is one of Thailand’s largest conglomerates, with businesses in telecoms, digital technology, retail, digital payments and media across 23 countries, and deep local resources and channels in Southeast Asia. Its subsidiary True Corporation is a leading Thai telecom and digital technology company, and TATANG by TrueID is True’s local microdrama platform, curating international microdramas for Thai audiences. Backed by CP Group’s consumer ecosystem in telecoms, payments, retail and brand marketing, TATANG is well placed to reach viewers.

The partnership moves forward on three fronts at once, content localization, talent development and distribution, connecting production, creation and distribution.

## Content: real localization, not just translation

COL and CP Group will adapt FlareFlow and other hit titles for Thailand. Rather than simply translating, the teams will rewrite characters, settings, dialogue and cultural context for Thai audiences, using AIGC in production and combining COL’s IP and microdrama expertise with CP Group’s local creator network and market resources.

## Talent: training creators with Thai universities

The two companies plan to work with leading Thai academic institutions on a microdrama talent program, training young local creators in storytelling, production and creative skills, and building a path from education into the industry.

## Distribution: a FlareFlow section on TATANG

FlareFlow Mini is now live on TATANG with its own FlareFlow section. Building on the CP and True consumer ecosystem, the partners will make FlareFlow content easier to find in Thailand and bring more localized microdramas to Thai viewers.

FlareFlow now has more than 52 million registered users worldwide. Starting from Thailand, the two companies will continue to expand localized content and distribution across Southeast Asia.
