Source: Hengqin Online

On the afternoon of October 21, Ho Hau Wah, Vice Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People’s Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) and Chairman of the second Advisory Committee of the Guangdong-Macao In-Depth Cooperation Zone in Hengqin (the “Cooperation Zone”), visited the Cooperation Zone. He toured Phase I of the Macao-Hengqin International Education (University) Town, the China–Portuguese-Speaking (Spanish-Speaking) Countries Economic and Trade Service Center, and Hengqin International Film and TV City, learning about higher-education cooperation and talent development between Hengqin and Macao, the trade center’s operations and services for companies going global, and the international expansion of cultural, creative, film, TV and digital content. He was accompanied by André Cheong Weng Chon, Executive Deputy Director of the Cooperation Zone’s Administrative Committee and Secretary for Administration and Justice of the Macao SAR Government.

At Phase I of the university town, Ho toured the campus and facilities and learned about the project’s construction, use of space, operations and the course offerings of the three universities. He heard a report from Pang Qifu, Director of the Cooperation Zone’s Talent Development and Social Security Bureau, on the university town and the progress of higher-education cooperation between Hengqin and Macao.

![Visiting Phase I of the Macao-Hengqin International Education (University) Town](2026-10-26-eqteiq/1.jpg)

At the China–Portuguese-Speaking (Spanish-Speaking) Countries Economic and Trade Service Center, Ho heard a report from Wu Yanxiang, Deputy Director of the Cooperation Zone’s Legal Affairs Bureau and Executive Director of the center, on the center’s operations, overseas service network, investment and financing support, and talent training.

The center aims to be a one-stop service platform connecting China with Portuguese- and Spanish-speaking countries. Under a “government guidance + market operation” model and a dual “online platform + offline park” service system, it offers one-stop services for going global, including legal and tax advice. It is backed by an RMB 1 billion investment fund for economic, trade and industrial development between China and Portuguese- and Spanish-speaking countries, and works closely with more than 200 professional service firms in China and abroad to meet companies’ needs as they expand overseas.

![Visiting the China–Portuguese-Speaking (Spanish-Speaking) Countries Economic and Trade Service Center](2026-10-26-eqteiq/2.jpg)

At Hengqin International Film and TV City, Ho visited the exhibition hall and learned about the city’s layout, facilities, construction and operations. Li Ming, Deputy General Manager of the Hengqin Microdrama Base, briefed him on the project’s plans, business model and overseas expansion.

![Visiting Hengqin International Film and TV City](2026-10-26-eqteiq/3.jpg)

During the visit, Ho encouraged Macao’s universities to seize the opportunity of extending their programs across the border and to draw on their international strengths to build a hub that integrates education, technology and talent. He praised the trade center’s progress so far and hoped it would develop in a complementary way with Macao, keep improving its service network, grow its professional team and, through sustained effort, deliver solid trade services for Portuguese- and Spanish-speaking countries, taking cooperation to a new level. He also hoped Hengqin International Film and TV City would link Macao’s international platform with Hengqin’s space, tell China’s stories well, help Chinese culture go global and enrich the culture, tourism and film industries of Hengqin and Macao.

Also accompanying the visit were Nie Xinping, Deputy Secretary of the Guangdong Provincial Party Committee’s Hengqin Working Committee, Director of the Guangdong Provincial Hengqin Office and Deputy Director of the Cooperation Zone’s Executive Committee; Zhang Guoji, Deputy Director of the Cooperation Zone’s Executive Committee; Song Yonghua, Rector of the University of Macau; Zhou Zhongrong, Rector of Macao Polytechnic University; Fanny Vong Chuk Kwan, President of Macao University of Tourism; and officials from the Cooperation Zone’s Executive Committee and the Macao SAR Government.
