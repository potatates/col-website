The 2026 Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Summit took place in Shenzhen on October 29–30. Focused on AI, AI agents and industrial digitalization, the summit brought ecosystem partners together to explore AI-driven opportunities for innovation. Invited as a Tencent Cloud digital content ecosystem partner, COL Group showcased joint work with Tencent Cloud in the Emerging Media Platforms exhibition area, covering AI animation production, global microdrama operations and enterprise AI agents, and showing how AI is empowering the digital content industry.

![](2026-11-01-aucbfg/1.jpg)

## AI content innovation across the full content chain

As generative AI advances rapidly, new content formats such as microdrama and AI animation are seeing fresh opportunities. AI is evolving from a creative assistant into a part of the entire process of content production, operations and monetization, bringing new momentum to quality and efficiency across the digital content industry.

At the summit, COL and Tencent Cloud presented several joint projects. In AI animation production, the two companies are building on Tencent Cloud’s TokenHub platform to explore unified compute scheduling and resource optimization, raising the efficiency of AI content production and supporting the large-scale development of quality IP across multiple formats.

For COL’s global microdrama business, the partners are working together on a real-time cloud data warehouse. Real-time business data supports scenarios such as overseas content marketing and user payment analysis, making global operations more efficient.

For microdrama distribution in China, COL has opened a dedicated content account in the microdrama section of WeChat, where it continues to release quality microdramas. Drawing on the reach of the Tencent ecosystem, this strengthens COL’s distribution network and helps quality content reach audiences more efficiently.

The two companies are also exploring enterprise AI agents. Using products such as WorkBuddy and WeCom, they are bringing AI into creator management, content production, rights management and financial settlement, with the aim of building a more efficient, intelligent system for content production and operations.

## Deepening the partnership with Tencent to grow AI-powered audiovisual content

Taking part in the Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Summit is another step in COL’s deepening partnership with the Tencent ecosystem and its push for innovation in AI content.

COL previously joined Tencent Cloud’s Tier-1 Club (T1 Club), working with ecosystem partners on collaborative innovation in AI-powered audiovisual content and bringing content resources, technology and industry use cases closer together.

During the summit, Tencent Cloud also hosted a session for emerging media platforms. The COL team was invited to join discussions on how AI and the digital content industry are converging, and on new approaches to content production, industry collaboration and business models in the AI era.

![](2026-11-01-aucbfg/2.jpg)

From large-scale AI animation production to global microdrama operations, and from smart tools to collaboration across the full content chain, AI is rapidly reshaping the digital content industry. Going forward, COL will continue its “AI First” strategy, drawing on its rich IP library, content ecosystem and global footprint to deepen cooperation with Tencent Cloud and other partners, apply AI across content creation, production and monetization, and open new room for the high-quality growth of the digital content industry.
